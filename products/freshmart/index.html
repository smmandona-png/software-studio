FreshMart

Simple stock and sales for the grocery shop owner.

FreshMart helps a shop owner see what is in stock, record what comes in and what is sold, and spot items that are running low.

Working prototype

Who it is for

Individual grocery shop owners who run one shop.

What it does

  • Two simple modes: Stock in and Sell
  • Add products with a photo
  • Speak to the app to record items
  • Quickly filter products that are low or out of stock
  • Settings to suit your shop
