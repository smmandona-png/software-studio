PowerPay

Buy prepaid electricity from your phone.

PowerPay is a prototype that lets a customer buy prepaid electricity and report faults from their phone, while staff follow sales and faults on their own dashboard.

Prototype

Who it is for

Electricity utilities and their customers.

What it does

  • Customer app: buy electricity and confirm the purchase
  • See your purchase history and account
  • Report a fault and check its progress
  • Staff dashboard: sales per day and sales by payment network
  • Staff dashboard: see reported faults and update their status
  • Staff dashboard: view customers
