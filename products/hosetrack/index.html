HoseTrack

Record every hydraulic hose failure and see where the problems start.

HoseTrack helps maintenance teams keep proper records of hydraulic hose failures, replacements and servicing, so the same breakdowns stop repeating.

Working app, version 1.1.0

Who it is for

Mining contractors, hydraulic workshops, mine maintenance departments and fleet operators.

What it does

  • Log a hose failure against a machine, with the hose type and size
  • Attach photos of the failed hose
  • Keep machine lists and your own choice lists
  • See reports with charts of what is failing
  • Export your records
  • Records are stored on the phone
  • Can be connected to a company system such as Pronto, through a service built by the company's IT team

Why it matters

Over time the records show which hoses fail repeatedly and where breakdowns come from, which supports preventative checks during servicing.
